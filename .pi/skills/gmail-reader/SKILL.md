# Gmail Reader Skill

## Usage
This skill gives you access to these tools:
- `list_emails` — list recent or unread emails
- `read_email` — read full content of a specific email
- `search_emails` — search emails by sender, subject, or keyword

## Example Tasks
- "show my unread emails"
- "read the latest email from my manager"
- "search for emails about the POC"
- "list my last 10 emails"

## Rules
- Always show sender, subject and date when listing emails
- Always confirm before doing anything destructive
- Keep email content concise when summarizing
